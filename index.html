Bakery & café · Tofino, BC

Naturally leavened bread, baked fresh on First Street.

The Common Loaf is Tofino’s bake shop — sourdough and naturally leavened breads, pastries and cakes from the oven, plus coffee and lunch, a short walk from the harbour. The day’s baking is posted fresh, so no two visits are quite the same.

From the oven & the counter

Sourdough & Breads

Naturally leavened loaves are the heart of the shop — crusty, slow-fermented bread baked in small batches. Arrive early; the good loaves go fast.

Pastries & Cakes

Sweet and savoury pastries, muffins and cakes, changing with the day and the season — the case is the menu, and it rewards regulars.

Coffee & Lunch

Espresso and coffee to stay or go, and simple lunch fare to carry down to the docks or eat in. Daily offerings are posted on Facebook each morning.
